A lab result can be valid HL7 and still be unsafe to route

  • The patient is not registered yet
  • The order number does not exist, or belongs to another patient
  • The lab resent a result that was already delivered
  • A required field is empty
  • The clinical record system is temporarily down
Interface teams investigate these by hand. Some must always reach a person.
